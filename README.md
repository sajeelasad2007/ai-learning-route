# AI Learning Route (installable app)

Your personal learning workspace for local AI and Python. It installs like an app on
Windows, laptops, Android and iPhone, and works offline.

## Put it online (free, one time, about 10 minutes)

1. Make a free account at https://github.com (skip if you have one).
2. Click **+** (top right) → **New repository**. Name: `ai-learning-route`.
   Choose **Public** (needed for free hosting). Click **Create repository**.
3. On the new page click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in ALL files from this
   folder: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, the four .png files, README.md.
   Click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**. Under "Build and deployment": Source **Deploy from a
   branch**, Branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**. Click **Save**.
5. Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh. The page shows your link:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/ai-learning-route/`

## Install it

- **Windows / laptop (Chrome or Edge):** open the link, click **Install app** in the page
  (or the install icon in the address bar).
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome → **Install app**.
- **iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Your data

- Saved on each device, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded; the public repository only
  holds the empty app, never your notes.
- To move or combine devices: **Export file** on one, **Import file → Merge** on the other.
- Export now and then as a backup (the app reminds you after 7 days).
- Sharing the link gives other people their own empty copy, not your data.

## Updating the app later

Upload the new index.html (and sw.js) to the same repository, replacing the old ones.
Devices pick up the new version the next time they open the app online.
